Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to the most common questions raised by our prospective partners and institutional investors.

What is the minimum capital required to partner with Fish Aqua Farming?

expand_more

Our partnership structures start at a minimum capital commitment of ₹10 Lakhs. This allows institutional and individual private partners to be allocated a biosecure, managed pond slice or hatchery unit.

What are the expected ROI timelines for a harvest cycle?

expand_more

Expected ROI timelines typically range between 12 to 18 months, depending on the cultivated species (e.g., Pangasius has a shorter cycle compared to some carp varieties). A full breakdown is provided in our luxury corporate presentation.

How is the profit-sharing contract executed?

expand_more

Profit sharing is executed directly into your registered bank account within 30 days of the harvest liquidation. All settlements are audited by third-party accounting firms, and partners receive complete invoice and sale transparency.

How do you protect the harvest against water/biological anomalies?

expand_more

We maintain state-of-the-art biosecurity protocols, including daily veterinary health checks, constant oxygen and water parameter testing, and automated backup aerators. However, standard force majeure clauses apply to environmental catastrophes.

Can partners physically visit or inspect the farm site?

expand_more

Yes, farm visits are highly encouraged as part of Step 3 of our journey. We arrange guided drone tours and physical inspection walk-throughs for all verified partners, maintaining strict disinfection and biosecurity entry rules.

What government approvals and compliance certifications do you hold?

expand_more

We hold a full stack of certifications including GST Registration, Incorporation documents, Pollution Control Board Clearance, MSME registration, ISO 9001:2015, and registrations under the National Aquaculture Authority. These are available in the Institutional Verification section.
