# **Premium Aquaculture Brand \- Website Requirement Specification**

This document outlines the comprehensive structure, visual aesthetics, features, and technical requirements for an international-standard, premium fish farming business website. The objective is to design a highly engaging, transparent, and professional digital experience to attract high-value investors and build absolute client trust.

## **1\. Interactive Landing Page (Hero Section)**

### **First Impression & Visuals**

> * **Full-Screen Immersive Background:** A highly realistic, premium 3D underwater animation.  
> * **Initial Focus:** High-quality visualization of healthy fish eggs (seeds) submerged in clear water.  
> * **Scroll-Triggered Animation:** The animation seamlessly progresses as the user scrolls down the page.

### **The Scroll Journey (3D Animated Steps)**

> * **Step 1:** The Fish Egg (Seed).  
> * **Step 2:** The egg hatches/cracks.  
> * **Step 3:** Baby Fish emerges.  
> * **Step 4:** Transition to Juvenile Fish.  
> * **Step 5:** Growing phase of the fish.  
> * **Step 6:** Adult Fish stage.  
> * **Step 7:** Large, fully healthy harvest-ready fish.  
> * **Step 8:** Final transition to a premium fish market or export transport truck.

*Dynamic Content Synchronization:* The text on the screen will automatically change to match the scroll stage. For example:

> * "Every successful harvest starts from a single healthy seed."  
> * "We nurture every stage with scientific care."  
> * "Finally delivering market-ready premium fish."
